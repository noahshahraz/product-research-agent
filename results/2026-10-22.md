## Details

### Cooling/breathable comforter or duvet insert, premium 'cloud' style, queen
- Angle: Use baffle-box construction, a breathable shell, and sleep-trial messaging against mid-market complaints.
- Fixable complaints: clumps or shifts, too hot, smells on arrival
- Requirements to sell: Textile fiber content labeling (FTC), RN/care labeling, and California TB117-2013 where applicable to filled bedding. Prop 65 warnings if relevant.
