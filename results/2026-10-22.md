## Details

### Hard water shower filter head with 2 spare cartridges (vitamin C/KDF)
- Angle: Sell a lightweight head with a longer-life cartridge and a refill pack (o-ring included), and state real cartridge lifespan.
- Fixable complaints: cartridge lasts only about a month, spray holes clog, head droops from filter weight, replacement cartridge missing o-ring
- Requirements to sell: Do not claim it treats skin conditions or eczema (drug claim). Water-treatment filtration claims (chlorine reduction) may need NSF/ANSI 177 testing to be stated; use 'filters' language only without certification. Standard Amazon category, no gating known.
- Notes: Duty is an estimate (~3% MFN + 25% legacy 301 + 12.5% new 301 assumed); HTS not verified. Unit cost is a guess, not a quote. Orders/customer 1.5 assumes ~half of buyers reorder cartridges in 12 months. Search interest reported +21% YoY (~37K/mo); market-size figures in sources conflict widely. Complaints come from reseller pages, not Amazon.

### Premium press-on nail kit (24 nails x several sets, glue and prep kit)
- Angle: Better size range, stronger adhesive tabs and clear fit guide.
- Fixable complaints: nails pop off in a day, sizing off, glue weak
- Requirements to sell: Cosmetic: MoCRA listing/exemption, adhesives need ingredient labels (watch for banned monomers like MMA); nail glue cyanoacrylate small bottle shipping rules.
- Notes: Wholesale cost found: $0.20-$1.50 per set, $0.55 on one Accio report; I used $1.80 for kit with glue. Revenue/customer ~ $41 depends on 2.3 orders, which is optimistic; borderline versus the $40 target.
- Sources: https://closo.co/blogs/inventory-liquidation/nails-wholesale https://smartbuy.alibaba.com/buyingguides/press-on-nails-in-bulk https://www.hostinger.com/ca/tutorials/?p=27202

### Pet enrichment bundle: silicone lick mat + fabric snuffle mat + slow feeder bowl
- Angle: Bundle three items with dishwasher-safe food-grade silicone and a feeding guide, rather than one commodity mat.
- Fixable complaints: mat tears or gets chewed, hard to clean fabric folds, single items feel overpriced
- Requirements to sell: Food-contact silicone: ask supplier for FDA LFGB/FDA food-grade test report. Pet products are not CPSIA children's products. State-level pet product labeling is minimal.
- Notes: Single lick mats retail ~$16 and wholesale as low as ~$5.5 (unverified aggregator), so the bundle is needed to clear ~$40/customer. Unit cost is a conservative guess. Pet enrichment flagged as growth niche by seller blogs; no hard numbers.
- Sources: https://www.accio.com/business/dog-lick-mat-trends https://amzprep.com/high-demand-amazon-products/ https://www.faire.com/product/p_wu3ffk7aaa

### Hydrocolloid pimple patch multipack (120 patches, 3 sizes)
- Angle: Better adhesion and a mixed shape pack; sell larger count for value.
- Fixable complaints: patches peel off, too small, no shape variety
- Requirements to sell: Claims of treating acne can make this an OTC drug; sell as 'blemish cover' with cosmetic-only claims or use an OTC monograph-compliant manufacturer (e.g., salicylic acid patches are drugs). MoCRA registration/listing may apply.
- Notes: Retail benchmark: 330-count at ~$8, Hero 72-count ~$16-21. Dominated by Hero (huge reviews). 2.4 orders is optimistic; borderline vs. $40 target. Cost is a guess.
- Sources: https://seller.alibaba.com/blogs/2026/southeast-asia/skincare/pimple-patch-manufacturing-packaging-quality-guide-alibaba-b2b https://camelcamelcamel.com/product/B0FN7KPF6Q https://www.hostinger.com/ca/tutorials/?p=27202

### Extra-thick non-slip yoga/exercise mat 6mm with strap
- Angle: TPE or natural rubber with odor-free finish and grip tested.
- Fixable complaints: slippery when sweaty, chemical smell, peeling top layer
- Requirements to sell: Prop 65, phthalate-free statement; no CPSIA unless marketed to kids; Amazon product safety documentation optional.
- Notes: Shopify notes yoga mats search peaks around January and rebounds toward year-end; steady not hot trend. Cost guessed. A lot of low-priced competitors.

### Microwavable weighted neck wrap + eye mask gift set
- Angle: Double-stitched flax-filled pockets and gift box with heating instructions.
- Fixable complaints: fill leaks or shifts, scent fades, overheats in microwave
- Requirements to sell: Not a medical claim product; avoid 'pain relief' claims. Labeling for textile fiber content (Textile Act) and heating instructions; no CPSIA unless marketed to kids.
- Notes: Retail anchors: $22.49 eye mask (Staples), $16-23 US promo neck wraps; factory cost unquoted (guess $5.50). Orders/customer 1.2 mostly gifting. Weaker trend evidence.
