# Product research: 2026-10-26

US tariffs on Chinese goods changed again: IEEPA duties were struck down in Feb 2026 and the 10% Section 122 surcharge lapsed July 24, 2026, but a new 12.5% Section 301 'forced-labor' duty now stacks on the 7.5%/25% legacy Section 301 lists plus MFN, so most China-sourced goods land around 20% to 40%+ (toys about 20%, apparel about 36.5%, general electronics and housewares about 37.5% to 40%). Amazon's January 2026 FBA fee increase and the April 2026 3.5% fuel surcharge raised fulfillment costs a little. Demand data (ASINSIGHT, July-Aug 2026) shows steady or rising sales in shower filters, scalp and hair care, cat fountains, magnetic tiles and water flossers, while pickleball paddles, weighted blankets, lip products and magnesium gummies fell sharply month over month.

Changes since last run: All 7 products from the 2026-10-22 run are kept and re-costed with the new duty stack. The yoga mat price is cut from $45 to $32 because the July 2026 top-10 sold at $19-$31; this lowers its margin and score. New entrants: heatless curl set, stainless cat water fountain, orthopedic dog bed, posture corrector, magnetic spice rack set, cordless water flosser, magnesium glycinate gummies, rosemary scalp-care kit, red light therapy wand, shiatsu neck massager, magnetic tiles, pickleball paddle set, resistance band kit, compression socks, toothpaste tablets, car trunk organizer, gua sha/ice roller kit, lip oil trio, weighted blanket, creatine gummies. Two new rejects (GLP-1 supplement, unlicensed Pokemon/World Cup merch). Trend scores rest on single-month MoM figures from ASINSIGHT, not 12-month Google Trends series, so treat them as moderate confidence. Cost, freight and CPC figures are my estimates; no live Alibaba quotes were available.

## Details

### Pet enrichment bundle: silicone lick mat + fabric snuffle mat + slow feeder bowl
- Angle: Bundle three matched pieces with dishwasher-safe, stronger-suction silicone and a feeding guide, priced above the $7 single mats.
- Fixable complaints: suction cups fail, mat tears when chewed, hard to clean snuffle fabric
- Requirements to sell: Food-contact silicone should be FDA 21 CFR 177.2600 compliant (LFGB/FDA test report from the factory) even though pet-use; no CPSIA needed. Avoid health claims ('cures anxiety').
- Notes: ASINSIGHT July 2026: top lick-mat listings $6-$17 selling 2K-7K/mo, several +50-100% MoM; best price point seen is $40.99 for a bundle-like listing selling 2K/mo. A US storefront quoted $10/unit at 500 units for custom lick mats (retail-style quote, higher than factory). My $6.00 bundle cost is an estimate. Pet feeder toys market projected 8% CAGR 2026-2034.

### Rosemary hair oil + scalp massager kit
- Angle: Pair a lightweight non-greasy oil with a silicone scalp brush in a gift box, selling the routine not a 'growth' promise.
- Fixable complaints: greasy, strong smell, no visible growth
- Requirements to sell: Cosmetic: MoCRA facility registration and product listing, safety substantiation, ingredient labeling. Do NOT claim 'regrows hair' or 'treats hair loss' (drug claim).
- Notes: ASINSIGHT July 2026: Mielle rosemary oil sold 60K/mo at $9.94 with 122K reviews and grew 100% MoM; others $10-$30 selling 6K-20K. Scalp massagers sell 4K-10K/mo at $5-$29 (one $12.99 at 2.5 stars). Customers repurchase oil, so 1.9 orders; assumes a 2-month supply. Duty: HTS 3305 List 4A + 12.5%.
- Sources: https://www.asinsight.com/report/US/rosemary-oil-for-hair-growth https://www.asinsight.com/report/US/scalp-massager https://www.tariffstool.com/tariffs-from-china

### Toothpaste tablets, 3-month supply in refill pouch
- Angle: Use a plastic-free pouch and a subscription that lines up with the 3-month supply.
- Fixable complaints: crumble in transit, chalky taste, glass jar breaks
- Requirements to sell: Fluoride toothpaste is an OTC drug (FDA monograph, drug listing, Drug Facts label). Fluoride-free tablets are cosmetics under MoCRA (facility registration, product listing). Do not claim to prevent cavities without fluoride.
- Notes: ASINSIGHT Mar 2026: NOBS 3-month pack at $38.24 sells about 10K/mo with 8.5K reviews, showing bundle pricing works. Assumes US contract manufacturer (duty 0); China sourcing would add about 0.2. No MoM data available.
- Sources: https://www.asinsight.com/report/US/toothpaste-tablets https://www.sellersprite.com/en/blog/amazon-fba-fees-2026-complete-guide https://explodingtopics.com/blog/find-trending-products

### Hard water shower filter head, 15-stage vitamin C/KDF, with 2 spare cartridges
- Angle: Ship a metal-swivel head with a thread-seal gasket and 3 cartridges, and add a reorder reminder card for a 2-3 month replacement cycle.
- Fixable complaints: cartridge only lasts about a month with heavy chlorine, pressure drops as filter clogs, leaks / cartridges don't seat, plastic housing durability
- Requirements to sell: Do not claim to treat skin conditions or remove contaminants beyond what is tested; NSF/ANSI 177 (vitamin C shower filter) is the only certification, so avoid 'certified' wording without it. If sold to Amazon, home category is open. Treat as water-treatment device marketing: no health claims. Replacement cartridges are a separate SKU for repeat sales.
- Notes: ASINSIGHT July 2026: top 10 sell 5-10K units/mo at $19-$43; two listings grew 100% MoM. Leaders have 12K-69K reviews, but a $38 listing sells 7K/mo with only 933 reviews, so entry is possible. Unit cost is my estimate for a 300-500 unit order (no live Alibaba quote); duty assumes HTS 8421.21 on Section 301 List 3 (25%) + 12.5% + MFN, about 0.40; verify with a broker. Repeat orders 1.4 assumed because many buyers never reorder cartridges.
- Sources: https://www.asinsight.com/report/US/shower-filter https://www.walmart.com/ip/232067455 https://www.lowes.com/reviews/sprite-ae7-bn-shower-heads/5016338675 https://www.tariffstool.com/tariffs-from-china

### Creatine monohydrate gummies, 60 count, US contract-manufactured
- Angle: Dose-transparent 5 g per serving with third-party lab results.
- Fixable complaints: underdosed creatine per gummy, sugary, too many gummies per serving
- Requirements to sell: Dietary supplement rules: FDA registration, cGMP, Supplement Facts, DSHEA claims only. Amazon requires third-party testing for supplements. Check gummy creatine dose labeling; some gummies underdeliver and draw FTC/FDA attention.
- Notes: ASINSIGHT July 2026: gummy listings at about 10K/mo each, leader is a capsule not a gummy. Market report claims 8-10% CAGR (promotional). Unit cost is my guess; low confidence on dose economics (5 g needs a big gummy count).

### Magnesium glycinate gummies, 60 count, US contract-manufactured
- Angle: Sell a sugar-reduced gummy with a clearly stated elemental magnesium dose and a 90-day subscription.
- Fixable complaints: gummies stick together, too sweet, low magnesium per serving
- Requirements to sell: Dietary supplement: FDA facility registration, cGMP (21 CFR 111) via the contract manufacturer, Supplement Facts label, structure/function claims only with the DSHEA disclaimer and FDA notification within 30 days. Amazon requires third-party testing for supplements.
- Notes: ASINSIGHT June 2026: top sellers 10K-80K/mo, but every product with a trend value was down 30-50% MoM, so the trend score is set to 5 pending a longer series. Assumes US manufacturing (duty 0); if made in China add about 0.20+. Repeat 2.2 orders/yr assumes some subscribers; a 30-day supply bought twice. Unit cost is an estimate from typical gummy contract quotes.
- Sources: https://www.asinsight.com/report/US/magnesium-glycinate-gummies https://www.asinsight.com/report/US/gummy-magnesium-glycinate https://www.sellersprite.com/en/blog/amazon-fba-fees-2026-complete-guide

### Microwavable weighted neck wrap + eye mask gift set
- Angle: Sell it as a gift set with a washable cover and unscented flax/clay fill.
- Fixable complaints: filling smells when heated, cover not removable, uneven heat
- Requirements to sell: Textile labeling; burn-risk warning label and heating instructions. No medical claims.
