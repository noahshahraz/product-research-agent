# Product research: 2026-11-02

The tariff picture changed since earlier runs: IEEPA duties ended in Feb 2026, a 10% Section 122 surcharge expired July 24, and China-origin goods now pay MFN + original Section 301 (25% Lists 1-3, 7.5% List 4A) + a new 12.5% Section 301 forced-labor tariff, so all duty rates here use that stack. Fibermaxxing, creatine, magnesium, K-beauty and supplements dominate current TikTok Shop and Google signals, and Q4 cozy/gifting items are in season. Evidence quality is mixed: Amazon Movers & Shakers and Reddit could not be fetched, and most unit costs are estimates from listing ranges, not supplier quotes.

Changes since last run: Carried over from the 2026-10-26 run: pet enrichment bundle, rosemary hair oil kit, toothpaste tablets, hard water shower filter, creatine gummies, magnesium gummies, neck wrap gift set. Duty rates were re-based to the post-IEEPA stack (12.5% Section 301 + Section 301 list rate where applicable); I did not re-derive the previous run's scores, so no score-move comparisons are claimed. New entrants this week: tallow balm, heatless curl set, electrolyte sticks, fiber gummies, quiet cat fountain, dog multivitamin chews, dog stroller, car organizer bundle, 3-in-1 charging station, wax warmer bundle, insulated tumbler, fleece leggings, kids craft kit, LED red light mask, humidifier, rechargeable hand warmers, pickleball set, wearable blanket hoodie, acupressure mat set, calming pet bed, gua sha set.
