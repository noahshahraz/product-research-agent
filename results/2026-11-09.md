## Details

### Hypochlorous acid face and body spray 2-pack, 3.4 oz
- Angle: Opaque, stable bottle with a dated expiry and cosmetic-only positioning.
- Fixable complaints: short shelf life, spray nozzle fails, no proof it works
- Requirements to sell: Cosmetic under MoCRA (facility registration, product listing, safety substantiation). Avoid 'treats acne/eczema' (drug) and 'disinfects/kills germs' (EPA pesticide registration under FIFRA). Amazon beauty may ask for documents.
- Notes: 5-year search growth +8,900% (Exploding Topics). Ulta comp: $27 per 4 oz. Private-label sources: Pravada MOQ 50-250; Alibaba-sourced listings $0.65-1.06/unit but unverified. I used $3 per unit contract-made in the US and 0% duty (US fill; imported components not included). Orders/year guess.
- Sources: https://explodingtopics.com/blog/trending-products https://pravadaprivatelabel.com/products/hypochlorous-acid-spray https://www.ulta.com/p/trust-me-on-this-hypochlorous-acid-spray-pimprod2053660

### Fluoride-free nano-hydroxyapatite toothpaste, 3-pack of 3.4 oz tubes
- Angle: Smooth 10% nHA formula in a 3-pack with a refill-by-mail option, compared against Boka/Apagard.
- Fixable complaints: gritty texture, price per tube, flavor is weak
- Requirements to sell: Fluoride-free toothpaste is generally a cosmetic: MoCRA facility registration, product listing, safety substantiation, ingredient labeling, adverse event reporting. Do not claim to treat or prevent cavities or remineralize enamel (drug claims). Fluoride versions are OTC drugs.
- Notes: 5-year search growth +6,200% (Exploding Topics). Supplier listings show $0.26-0.70 per tube (MOQ 72-10,000), but those are bare tubes; I used $2.50 per tube for a contract-made, boxed product. 1.8 orders/yr is a guess; a tube lasts ~6 weeks. Duty guess is 7.5% List 4A + 12.5%.
- Sources: https://explodingtopics.com/blog/trending-products https://www.alibaba.com/discover/teeth-cleansing.html https://www.cinoll.com/faqs/

### Peptide eye cream, 0.5 oz, 2-pack
- Angle: Airless pump, fragrance-free formula, 2-pack sized for 3 months.
- Fixable complaints: irritates eyes, pump clogs, small amount for price
- Requirements to sell: Cosmetic under MoCRA: facility registration and product listing, safety substantiation, label ingredients. No claims to repair collagen or treat wrinkles at a structural level (drug claims). Expect Amazon beauty gating.
- Notes: Exploding Topics: 6,600/mo, +325%. Private-label quotes conflict: $2.40-4.80 at 500u in one guide vs $4-22 in another; I used $6 per jar x2. Orders/yr 1.8 is a guess. Heavily competed by established brands.
- Sources: https://explodingtopics.com/product-topics https://xiranskincare.com/top-10-private-label-peptide-skincare-manufacturers/ https://www.pravadaprivatelabel.com/products/advanced-firming-eye-cream
