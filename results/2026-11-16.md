## Details

### Hypochlorous acid face and body spray 2-pack, 3.4 oz
- Angle: Opaque, stable bottle with a dated expiry and cosmetic-only positioning against Briotech and Ulta brands.
- Fixable complaints: short shelf life, spray nozzle fails, no proof it works
- Requirements to sell: Cosmetic under MoCRA (facility registration, product listing, safety substantiation, ingredient labeling). Avoid 'treats acne/eczema' (drug) and 'disinfects/kills germs' (likely EPA FIFRA pesticide claims). Amazon beauty may require documents.
- Notes: Exploding Topics: 148,400 searches/mo, +8,900% over 5 years. Pravada US private label: MOQ 50-250 units, sample $8.45, no per-unit price published. Marketplace China listings $0.65-1.06/unit (unverified). I assumed $3/unit US contract-filled (2-pack = $6) and 0% duty because it is US-filled; imported components are not counted. Orders/yr 1.8 is a guess (unit lasts ~2 months). Competition and complaint scores are judgment, not measured review counts.

### Fluoride-free nano-hydroxyapatite toothpaste, 3-pack of 3.4 oz tubes
- Angle: Smooth 10% nHA formula in a 3-pack with a refill option, positioned against Boka and Apagard.
- Fixable complaints: gritty texture, price per tube, weak flavor
- Requirements to sell: Fluoride-free toothpaste is generally a cosmetic: MoCRA facility registration, product listing, ingredient labeling. Do not claim to prevent cavities or remineralize enamel (drug claims). Fluoride versions are OTC drugs.
- Notes: Exploding Topics: 43,300 searches/mo, +6,200% over 5 years. Chinese listings $0.19-0.80/tube at MOQ 20-10,000 (bare product, no tube/box/landed cost). Cinoll says it treats fluoride-free toothpaste as a MoCRA cosmetic (supplier claim). I used $2.50/tube boxed. Duty = assumed 7.5% List 4A + 12.5%; HTS list status not confirmed. Orders/yr 1.8 is a guess.
- Sources: https://explodingtopics.com/blog/trending-products https://www.alibaba.com/discover/teeth-cleansing.html https://www.cinoll.com/faqs/

### Creatine monohydrate gummies, 60 count (private label)
- Angle: Third-party-tested gummies with the full 5 g dose per serving and a clean sugar profile.
- Fixable complaints: less creatine than label, sugar content, sticky gummies
- Requirements to sell: Dietary supplement: cGMP (21 CFR 111), FDA facility registration, Supplement Facts panel, no disease claims, adverse event reporting. Amazon requires supplement category approval and third-party test reports. Imported food-type goods need FDA prior notice.
- Notes: Exploding Topics: 117,700/mo, +5,200% over 5 years. Alibaba listings show $1.50-5.00 per unit at MOQ ~100 boxes to 2,000 bottles; claims of FDA/NSF are seller assertions. Many gummies fail the dose claim (5 g does not fit in a gummy), which is also a lawsuit risk. Competition is dominated by big brands. Duty guess.
- Sources: https://explodingtopics.com/blog/trending-products https://www.alibaba.com/product-introduction/Monohydrate-Creatine-Gummies-Private-Label-Creatine_1601244654448.html https://wonnda.com/product-types/private-label-creatine/

### Peptide eye cream, 0.5 oz, 2-pack
- Angle: Airless pump, fragrance-free formula, 2-pack sized for about three months.
- Fixable complaints: irritates eyes, pump clogs, small amount for the price
- Requirements to sell: Cosmetic under MoCRA: facility registration, product listing, safety substantiation, ingredient labeling. No claims to repair collagen or treat wrinkles (drug claims). Expect Amazon beauty gating.
- Notes: Exploding Topics: 6,600/mo, +325%. Carried from 2026-11-09; private-label quotes conflict ($2.40-4.80 vs $4-22 per jar). I used $6/jar for a US contract-made, boxed jar; 0% duty assumes US fill. Heavily competed by established brands.
